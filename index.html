Work

  • Dynamo Lab, UCSB 2024 – now

    My research is on mechanistic interpretability for materials discovery. We analyze models' representation spaces to find directions or manifolds that encode physical properties, and steer along them to generate materials with target properties. Steering has proven effective for first-order properties like atomic density. Currently, I'm looking at steering for more complex properties like formation energy and band gap.

    Before that: reconstructing images from fMRI with latent diffusion on the THINGS dataset (see reconstructions), and predicting neural responses from multimodal data (CLIP, Whisper, InternVL).
